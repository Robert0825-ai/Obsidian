# 生成提示词

日期：2026-08-15  
用法：附上锁图 → 复制下面**一整段英文**（不要东拼西凑说明书）。会动的帧：锁图 + 上一帧 +「动画：在上一帧上改」。  
谁长什么样：[[角色设定]]。规矩从哪来：文末「这些提示词在防什么」。

把第一句 `SUBJECT: ...` 改成这次要画的东西即可。

## 静帧 · 通用（植物、建筑、道具、立绘都用这段）

```
SUBJECT: the attached lock image
Pixel art, same design as the lock. Same colors, same outline thickness, same zoom, same size.
Flat background #888888, lots of empty gray padding. No text, no extra characters, no extra props.

Do NOT recolor, desaturate, or restyle. Do NOT zoom in or out. Do NOT make it bigger or smaller than the lock.
Same camera angle as the lock. If it has a face: side profile FACING RIGHT, head on the right, do NOT mirror, do NOT flip.
Change only what this request asks. Everything else stays identical to the lock.
One subject only.
```

## 动画 · 在上一帧上改（第 2 帧及以后）

```
This is the NEXT frame of the same animation as the two attached images (lock + previous frame).
Pixel art, background #888888. Same subject, same colors, same outline, same zoom, same size, same facing.

This frame must look 90% identical to the previous frame. Move only the moving parts by a few pixels.
More frames means SMALLER motion, not a new pose. If it has feet or paired limbs: keep the same contact for TWO frames before switching which side is forward.

Last frame of a loop must connect to the first: no size pop, no palette shift, no mirrored facing, no part jumping to a new place.
FORBIDDEN: random pose; one frame much larger or smaller; debug colors (cyan, yellow, magenta) in the final art.
```

## 四足动物（猫、狸花、狗、兽）

### 待机

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same colors, same size, same outline as the lock. No collar unless the lock has one. No extra props.

IDLE: same silhouette every frame. Only a tiny idle motion (breath, tail tip, or ear). Do NOT change body size. Do NOT stand up if the lock is sitting. Do NOT sit down if the lock is standing.
Tail or extra parts stay near their lock height; the last frame must match the first. FORBIDDEN: a part suddenly much higher on the last frame.
Do NOT zoom. One animal only.
```

### 走路 · 近侧后脚在后（Contact A）

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT, standing walk (not sitting). Background #888888. Same colors, same body size, same zoom as the lock.

WALK Contact A: NEAR hind foot planted FAR BACK; FAR hind foot planted FORWARD under the belly. Visible gap between the two hind feet.
NEAR front foot forward, FAR front foot back. Diagonal gait, not pacing.
HIND LEGS MUST ALTERNATE. FORBIDDEN: both hind legs glued, stacked, or sliding as a pair; same foot forward every frame; mirroring; debug-colored legs.

Keep this contact for TWO frames before changing feet. Tail at a stable medium-low height, same every walk frame.
One animal only.
```

### 走路 · 近侧后脚在前（Contact B，和上一张相反）

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT, standing walk. Background #888888. Same colors, same body size as the lock and the previous walk frame.

WALK Contact B, OPPOSITE hind pose: NEAR hind foot planted FORWARD under the belly; FAR hind foot planted FAR BACK. Huge gap between hind feet.
NEAR front foot back, FAR front foot forward.
This is the other half of the walk cycle. Do NOT copy Contact A legs. Do NOT glue the hind legs. Do NOT mirror. Do NOT change size or colors.
Hold TWO frames, then change feet a little.
```

### 路过（两脚交叉的中间帧）

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same size and colors as the previous walk frame. Look 90% identical.

WALK passing pose: hind legs crossing under the hip. One hind foot lifting and swinging forward a few pixels, the other hind foot planted. Front legs mid-swap.
Do NOT glue hind feet. Do NOT sit. Do NOT zoom. Do NOT recolor.
```

### 冲刺

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same colors as the lock.

DASH: body stays LOW and STRETCHED every frame, same length, same size. Hind legs still alternate: one hind foot a step ahead of the other. Front feet staggered.

FORBIDDEN: bunching into a ball; squash then pop; both hind legs glued; sudden shrink; mirror.
Hold each foot pose TWO frames, then switch hind lead a little. Tail trailing at the same height every dash frame.
One animal only.
```

### 滑铲 / 贴地短动作

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same colors, same size as the lock.

SLIDE: 2 or 3 frames only. Same LOW pancake pose every frame. Belly near the ground, front feet stretched forward, hind feet stretched back, tail low.
FORBIDDEN: sitting up; high crouch; bouncing between flat and tall; extra frames with a different height.
Only the tail tip may move a few pixels.
```

### 跳（3 帧，播一次）

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same colors, similar body mass as the lock. Three frames only:

1) TAKEOFF: hips down, hind legs coiled, still on the ground.
2) AIR: stretched or slightly tucked, NOT a tiny ball, NOT a different sized animal.
3) LAND: paws down, slight impact.

Do not put a jump pose inside an idle loop. Do NOT mirror. One animal only.
```

### 爬

```
SUBJECT: quadruped animal matching the attached lock
Pixel art, side profile FACING RIGHT. Background #888888. Same colors, same body size. No ladder, no pole, no wall prop.

CLIMB: upright reach. Two frames per reach. HIND LEGS ALTERNATE: one hind foot down, the other tucked up, then swap. Front feet reach up, staggered, not glued.
Tail stable, no sudden high curl. Do NOT change size. One animal only.
```

## 场景地块 · 草皮盖土（空洞骑士的「模样」，猫的「笔」）

要的是空洞骑士苍绿之径那种**结构**：平台顶上一层活草/苔，下面是潮土，顶边可以略不平、草可稍微垂过沿。  
不要的是空洞骑士的**笔法**：手绘软边、雾画进砖里、几十种绿。  
笔法跟 **A 暂定皮**：1 像素硬黑描边、平涂、每种颜色最多 3 档、真像素格。锁图仍附 `A_暂定皮.png`（只借描边粗细和像素感，不要把猫画进砖里）。

### 横版草土地砖（32×32，可拼）

```
SUBJECT: one sidescroller ground TILE, 32x32 pixels exactly, for a 2D platformer
Style lock: the attached cat image. Copy ONLY its craft: 1-pixel hard charcoal-BLACK outline, visible pixel grid, cel-shading with at most 3 tones per color. Do NOT copy the cat into the tile. No anti-alias, no blur, no painterly brush, no photo texture.

STRUCTURE like a lush Hollow Knight Greenpath ledge (structure only, original art, do NOT copy any Hollow Knight asset): a thin CAP of living grass/moss on TOP, warm damp DIRT as the body below. Grass slightly overhangs the front edge. Top surface a little uneven, still a walkable platform.

Palette, lush and alive, not gray, not neon:
- grass: emerald, yellow-green highlight, deep green shadow (3 greens max)
- dirt: warm brown, darker brown in shadow, a little green-brown where soil is damp (3 browns max)
Outline same weight as the attached cat.

Side-view cross-section (not top-down RPG). Seamless left-right so tiles can sit in a row. Transparent background OR flat #888888 with lots of padding, no sky painting, no fog, no particles baked in.
No characters, no text, no flowers unless asked, no cyan/magenta debug.
```

## 这些提示词在防什么

| 踩过的坑 | 写进上面哪段 |
|----|----|
| 后腿并排、不交替 | 四足走路 / 冲刺 / 爬 |
| 颜色一帧一个样 | 每一段都有 same colors, not gray |
| 忽大忽小 | same size / 90% identical |
| 末帧朝左 | FACING RIGHT, do NOT mirror |
| 末帧零件跳位 | last frame matches first |
| 帧多了姿势乱跳 | TWO frames then switch; 90% identical |
| 冲刺缩成一团 | DASH stretched, no ball |
| 滑铲又扁又高 | SLIDE 2–3 frames, same low pose |
| 场景画成空洞骑士原画 / 和猫两套笔 | 场景地块：结构学苍绿之径，笔法锁猫的描边平涂 |

自检：整张表含末帧，颜色、大小、朝向、成对肢体是否交替。还怪先改他指出的那一张。

## 自检要「量」，不能只用眼睛看（2026-08-15 补）

眼睛看单帧会骗人：每一帧四条腿都在、都有姿势，整圈放起来还是「猫在地上滑」。跑 `firebot/tools/audit_cat_frames.py`，要出 `AUDIT_OK`。

| 量什么 | 怎么算 | 合格线 |
|----|----|----|
| 后腿有没有交替 | 两只后爪的间距，整圈的**摆幅** | ≥ 8% 身长。walk 现在 `230→64→274→23`，摆幅 31% |
| 这只猫多大 | 身上**不透明像素**的数量开根号 | 同动作内差 ≤ 6% |
| 有没有被拉长压扁 | 宽高比 | 同动作内差 ≤ 22% |
| 脚底齐不齐 | 最低一行不透明像素 | 同动作内差 ≤ 4px |
| idle 尾巴 | 屁股那侧 30% 宽里最高的像素 | 跳动 ≤ 身高 10%；末帧对第一帧 ≤ 5% |

**体量千万不要量包围盒。** 跳跃空中帧那只猫画小了一号却过了检查，因为它斜着放，包围盒被撑大、面积看着正常。数像素才骗不了。

**跨动作也要量。** 同一动作内部对齐还不够：新画的 idle1 比 idle2 小 28%，两个动作各自都很稳，一切换猫就当场大一圈。新动作要在 `build_cat_a_frames.py` 的 `SCALE_ANCHOR` 里指一个**已经认过的同姿势动作**当锚点。
不能把所有动作一起按面积归一化——坐着的猫腿收在身子底下，剪影面积本来就比站着小。也别指望找一把「跟姿势无关的自动尺子」：眼睛（红耳朵会混进去）、白爪（同动作内爪高在 17~29 飘）都试过，不准。看 `sheets/compare_actions.png` 用眼睛过一遍。

**帧数 ≠ 姿势数。** 8 帧 = 4 个姿势各占 2 帧；6 帧 = 3 个姿势。只有 2 个姿势会变成闪烁（dash 曾经是 `A A B B A A`）。3 帧的 slide 也不能三张一模一样，那等于静止图——留 2 个姿势，其中一张只动尾巴尖。

## 生成模型的老毛病（先写进提示词堵住）

| 它会自己跑偏成什么 | 提示词里要加什么 |
|----|----|
| 立起来 / 挂着的姿势被拉细拉长 | `same torso thickness as the attached frame; do NOT draw a thin stretched cat` |
| 「贴地滑」画成趴着睡觉、头贴地闭眼 | `head UP and alert, eye looking FORWARD, ears swept back` |
| 空中帧画成一只更小的猫 | `exactly the SAME SIZE as the attached frame; do NOT zoom out; body level, not tilted` |
| 两只后腿并成一对 | `staggered, one hind paw clearly ahead of the other, never side by side` |

顺序帧一张一张生成，把**上一帧一起当参考**；不要并行生成同一循环里相邻的两帧。
